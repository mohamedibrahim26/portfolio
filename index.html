Jan 2026 – Jun 2026 · MSc Thesis · Jönköping University
Indicators and Practices for Assessing Cybersecurity Awareness in SMEs

PRISMA-based Systematic Literature Review screening 4,510 papers (3,754 after deduplication) across IEEE Xplore, ACM, Scopus, and SpringerLink; 52 studies selected. Identified and classified cybersecurity awareness indicators into four categories (self-reported, behavioural, organisational, knowledge-based) and uncovered a significant research gap in SME-focused measurement frameworks.

⎈ GitHub
2026 · KTH ASSERT-lab PhD Recruitment Task
Natural Language Autoencoders on a Small Open Model

Faithful small-scale reimplementation of Anthropic's Natural Language Autoencoder paper on Qwen2.5-0.5B, conducted as a KTH ASSERT-lab task under Prof. Monperrus. Trained a paired verbalizer and reconstructor using LoRA, achieving FVE of 3.0% on generated descriptions vs –6.5% for random text. A paraphrase faithfulness test confirmed the bottleneck carries genuine semantic content: surface rewrites left FVE essentially unchanged (0.032 vs 0.030), reproducing the paper's qualitative confabulation finding at small scale.

Orion-XAI: Fine-Tuned LLM for Explainable AI in Cybersecurity

Full-stack AI chat application built around a custom fine-tuned model: Llama 3.2 3B trained with LoRA on 2,918 instruction examples covering SHAP, LIME, and Anchors for SOC decision support.
